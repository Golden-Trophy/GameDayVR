ABOUT THE GAME

What is Game Day VR?

Game Day VR is a virtual reality sports game built around simple, fun gameplay and playing with other people.

The idea is to take the feeling of sitting down with friends to play a sports game and bring it into VR.

It's inspired by games like Wii Sports and the social atmosphere of games such as Rec Room, while aiming to create its own world and identity.
